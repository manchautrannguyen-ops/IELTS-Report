Task Response 5 The task requires the writer to discuss two specific views — that education can solve the junk food problem, and that it cannot — and then give a personal opinion. Both sides are touched upon, which is a positive. However, the "education can work" paragraph drifts significantly off-topic. Much of it discusses physical activity and sport competitions rather than education about diet, and the connection between these ideas and the central argument is not made clear. The "education will not work" section is more relevant, arguing that personal choice and the convenience of junk food override educational awareness — these are valid points but are underdeveloped. The personal opinion in the conclusion ("keep the balance") is vague and not defended anywhere in the essay. The task is only incompletely addressed, which aligns with Band 5.
Coherence & Cohesion 5 There is a visible attempt at structure — two body paragraphs and a conclusion — and some connective devices are used ("Besides," "Thanks to this way," "Another way," "On the other hands," "For example"). However, cohesion within paragraphs breaks down frequently. The first body paragraph jumps between school lessons, parent communication, physical activities, and sport competitions without a clear unifying argument. "On the other hands" is a fixed phrase error — the correct form is "on the other hand." Sentences are not always fluently linked, and the relationship between ideas sometimes has to be inferred rather than being made explicit. Paragraphing is present but the central focus of the first body paragraph is unclear.
Lexical resource 5 There is some range of vocabulary: "consequences," "convenient," "maintain," "physical activities", but errors and imprecisions are frequent. "help we help quick meal" (incomprehensible), "eat its for long time" (eating it for a long time), "some reality activities" (should be "practical activities" or "hands-on activities"), "celebrated" used where "held" or "organised" is needed, "cheating meal" (likely a translation of "cheat meal" but used awkwardly), "eat alot" (spelling — "a lot"), "go restaurant" should be "go to restaurants", "Some times" should be "Sometimes", "feeling want to eat" and "stay it in balance for good life" are awkward phrasing, "Specially" (especially), "Concluding" (in conclusion). These errors are noticeable and occasionally impede meaning, characteristic of Band 5.
Grammatical range & accuracy 4–5 There is a mix of simple and complex sentences, but the complex structures are frequently faulty. Key errors include: "help we help quick meal" (pronoun error), "eat its for long time", "student are taught" ("students are"), "many kind of food" (plural agreement — "many kinds"), "eat healthy and unhealthy" (wrong word form: eat healthily and unhealthily), "Some sport competitions can celebrated" (missing auxiliary — "can be celebrated"), "education do not work" (subject-verb agreement — "does not work"), "eating play a important role" (agreement error and wrong article: "plays an important role"), "it depend on themselves" (agreement: "depends on"). Some sentence structures break down altogether ("is eating junk food that is", "do not eat"). Punctuation is generally adequate. The frequency of these errors at the sentence level is consistent with Band 4–5.
Overall estimated band5.0
Key areas to improve
  • Keep the "education can work" paragraph focused on educational measures specifically — school lessons, awareness campaigns, nutrition curricula — and remove the physical activity content, which is a separate topic.
  • The personal opinion must be stated clearly in the introduction and supported in the essay, not saved for a vague one-line conclusion.
  • Work on subject-verb agreement, especially with third-person singular ("education does not work," "it depends").
  • Replace "On the other hands" with "On the other hand" — this is a fixed expression.
  • "Concluding" is not a standard essay conclusion marker — use "In conclusion" instead.
