Nhận xét bài Writing

Học viên: Nhật Huy  ·  Điểm ước tính: 7

Task

Scientists agree that many people eat too much junk food and it is damaging their health. Some people think that this problem can be solved by education, while others believe that this education will not work. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Essay

Many scientists believe that excessive consumption of fast food harms individuals' well-being. While some people argue that this issue can be tackled through education, others think the educational approaches are ineffective. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I lean towards the latter view.

On the one hand, education can play a crucial role in reducing unhealthy food choices. First, it may help consumers gain more knowledge about the negative impacts of junk food like fried chicken and hamburger. For instance, school can teach students about nutrition and chronic diseases like obesity, diabetes and heart issues. In this way, schools can raise awareness for their students and let them make their own healthy food choices. In addition, early education shapes healthy eating habits for people in their childhood which can last too long. When children are continually exposed to nutritious instruction, they might develop a preference for healthy foods over processed foods. This will promote healthy eating habits and improve their overall health.

On the other hand, education is insufficient to solve these problems due to some reasons. First, junk food is easily accessible and convenient, which makes it hard for consumers to neglect it. For example, many famous fast food chains like KFC and Jollibee emerged densely in urban areas and provide ready-made meals that are significantly cheaper than traditional dishes. These reasonable prices force low-income labor to prioritize affordability over long-term health and nutritious food. Secondly, large food companies invest much money in marketing strategies that influence consumer choices, especially among young people. It makes their products more eye-catching and delicious. Therefore, knowledge does not affect this action.

In conclusion, although educational methods can promote a healthy lifestyle and may improve well-being, it is not enough to solve the problems of fast food because of affordability and strong marketing strategies. Therefore, I believe that more measures like the government's regulations on advertising should be introduced to address this problem.

Hướng cải thiện
  • Cần đẩy mạnh quan điểm cá nhân hơn. Hiện tại bài chủ yếu đang trình bày hai phía khá cân bằng, nhưng chưa thật sự đi sâu vào việc vì sao người viết cho rằng giáo dục là chưa đủ.
  • Về từ vựng, cần dùng chính xác hơn. Có một số từ nhìn thì đúng nhưng chưa tự nhiên, như 'reasonable prices', 'neglect', 'last too long'. Nên đọc thêm các bài viết chất lượng để quen với cách dùng từ và collocation chuẩn.
  • Cách dùng từ nối cần đa dạng hơn. Hiện tại vẫn phụ thuộc vào kiểu liệt kê như 'first', 'secondly'. Có thể dùng những cách nối tự nhiên hơn như 'more critically', 'what is particularly significant', 'this is compounded by' để bài viết mượt và tinh tế hơn.
  • Ngoài ra, cần chú ý đến đại từ. Khi dùng 'it' hoặc 'this', phải đảm bảo người đọc hiểu rõ nó đang chỉ cái gì, tránh gây mơ hồ.
CriterionScoreComment
Task Response 7 The essay addresses both views clearly and maintains a consistent position throughout. The introduction signals the writer's opinion, and the conclusion reinforces it with a practical suggestion. Ideas are extended and supported with relevant examples: KFC/Jollibee for accessibility, chronic diseases for education's benefits. However, there are some weaknesses that prevent a Band 8: the opinion ("I lean towards the latter view") is stated but not strongly argued in its own right. The essay mostly presents the two sides rather than actively defending the writer's position.
Coherence & Cohesion 7 There is clear overall progression and logical organisation throughout. Paragraphs have clear central topics and internal sequencing is generally logical. Cohesive devices are used with reasonable flexibility: while, in addition, first, secondly, therefore, although, though there is some over-reliance on listing connectors (first... secondly) rather than more sophisticated logical linking. One noticeable issue: "it is not enough to solve the problems of fast food" in the conclusion is slightly imprecise. It is unclear whether "problems" refers to junk food consumption or health problems caused by it. Minor lapses like this prevent a Band 8.
Lexical resource 7 There is a solid range of vocabulary used with reasonable precision: excessive consumption, nutritious instruction, densely emerged, affordability, marketing strategies, processed foods. The writer shows awareness of collocation and attempts less common items. However, some word choices are imprecise or slightly awkward: "neglect it" (avoid/resist would be more natural), "reasonable prices" (used where low prices is intended; "reasonable" implies positive value), "habits can last too long" (should be last a lifetime or persist into adulthood), "eye-catching and delicious" (delicious is oddly placed here as a marketing descriptor). Spelling appears error-free, which is a clear improvement over previous essays.
Grammatical range & accuracy 7 A good variety of complex structures is used and generally controlled: relative clauses, conditional-style structures, and multi-clause sentences appear throughout. Most sentences are error-free. A few issues persist: "due to some reasons" is an awkward filler phrase better omitted, "invest much money" should be invest a great deal of money, "knowledge does not affect this action" is unclear and grammatically weak, "it makes their products" has an ambiguous pronoun reference. These are minor and do not impede communication, but they are consistent enough to keep the essay at Band 7 rather than 8.
Overall estimated band7
What Would Push This to Band 7.5–8
  • Strengthen your own opinion. Rather than just presenting two balanced sides, dedicate more space to why you personally find education insufficient. Your conclusion introduces the government regulation idea well, but it would carry more weight if briefly set up in the body.
  • Refine word choice precision. Watch for words that almost fit but miss slightly — reasonable prices, neglect, last too long. Reading high-quality English articles and noting collocations will help here.
  • Vary your cohesive devices. Move beyond first/secondly toward more nuanced connectors like more critically, what is particularly significant, this is compounded by to show greater sophistication.
  • Tighten pronoun references. When you use it or this, make sure the noun it refers to is unambiguous to any reader.

Recommendations
  • Articles and prepositions are the highest-frequency error type and the easiest to drill systematically. Specifically: "the Apple" → "Apple", "focus in" → "focus on", "use it in many years" → "use it for many years". These are not complex grammar; they are habit errors that repeated correction can fix.

  • Leverage the vocabulary advantage: This candidate already has the lexical range to reach Band 6. The grammar consistency is what needs to catch up, making targeted error correction more efficient than broader vocabulary study at this stage.
