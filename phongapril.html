Section 3.2: Feedback for Writing

Nhận xét bài Writing

Học viên: Trung Phong  ·  Điểm ước tính: 6.5

Task

The availability of entertainment such as video games on handheld devices are harmful to individuals and to the society they live in. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Essay

Nowadays, smartphones and tablets are very popular. Many people think that having entertainment like video games on these handheld devices is harmful for individuals and the whole society. I believe that while this trend does have some negative impacts, it is not completely harmful.

On the one hand, spending too much time on mobile entertainment can bring significant problems. Firstly, individuals can easily become addicted to these digital activities, such as video games. For instance, many teenagers spend all their free time looking at the screen instead of doing homework or playing outdoor sports which is very harmful for their physical health. Moreover, it is also bad for society. When people focus too much on their handheld devices, they do not want to communicate with others face to face. In fact, it is common to see groups of friends in coffee shops just looking at their phones instead of talking to each other. As a result, they become gradually isolated from the real world.

On the other hand, mobile entertainment on handheld devices can also bring several positive effects. The most important benefit is that it helps people to relax. After a long day of hard work or studying at school, watching a funny video or playing a short game on a mobile phone is a very convenient way to reduce stress. These forms of entertainment can be enjoyed anywhere, such as while waiting for a bus. Furthermore, certain entertainment apps and video games are beneficial for the mind because they help people learn new skills, including English or problem-solving. In addition, users have the chance to make friends online, which is a modern way to connect with others.

In conclusion, while the availability of entertainment on handheld devices has some negative impacts like addiction and a lack of real-life communication, I strongly believe that if people know how to limit their screen time, it is a very useful tool to relieve stress and learn new things.

Cả hai phía đều được đề cập với những ý phù hợp: nghiện và sự cô lập xã hội ở khía cạnh tiêu cực; giảm căng thẳng, học tập và kết nối trực tuyến ở khía cạnh tích cực. Các ý được phát triển ở mức khá với ví dụ cụ thể (teenagers neglecting homework, friends on phones in coffee shops, waiting for a bus). Điểm yếu chính là sự mất cân đối nhẹ: phần phản đối thuyết phục và chi tiết hơn phần ủng hộ. Kết bài có sự biến đổi nhẹ về thái độ truyền tải: từ "not completely harmful" sang "strongly believe it is very useful" (thiếu nhất quán). Thay vì "strongly believe it is very useful", nên dùng "believe it can be useful".

Các từ nối khá đa dạng và nhìn chung được sử dụng phù hợp: "Firstly," "Moreover," "In fact," "As a result," "Furthermore," "In addition," "In conclusion." Tuy nhiên, một số từ chuyển ý còn hơi máy móc. Ví dụ, "In addition" trước ý về online friends không thể hiện được mối liên kết logic với ý trước đó. Việc sử dụng phép tham chiếu và thay thế nhìn chung ổn, không gây khó hiểu.

Tuy nhiên, so với band 7, bài viết vẫn còn sử dụng vốn từ chung chung và an toàn. Các cụm như "very popular," "very harmful," "very convenient," và "very useful" xuất hiện lặp lại nhiều lần, chưa dùng được những từ chính xác hơn thay cho "very + adj".
Để lên band 7, nên sử dụng các cách diễn đạt tự nhiên và học thuật hơn, ví dụ:
"sedentary lifestyle" thay cho "harmful for their physical health"
"diminishes face-to-face interaction" thay cho "do not want to communicate with others face to face"

Học viên sử dụng tốt mệnh đề quan hệ ("which is a modern way to connect with others"), câu điều kiện ("if people know how to limit their screen time"), và cụm phân từ ("watching a funny video or playing a short game").
Câu "many teenagers spend all their free time looking at the screen instead of doing homework or playing outdoor sports which is very harmful for their physical health" có mệnh đề quan hệ hơi mơ hồ. Tuy nhiên, nhìn chung, lỗi sai khá nhỏ và không xuất hiện nhiều.

Các điểm cần cải thiện chính
  • Giảm dùng "very + adjective": Thay bằng từ mạnh và chính xác hơn, hoặc tốt hơn là chọn một tính từ cụ thể hơn thay vì dùng very.
  • Đẩy vốn từ học thuật nhiều hơn: Sử dụng từ vựng chính xác, cụ thể sẽ giúp cải thiện Lexical Resource đến Band 7.
CriterionScoreComment
Task Response 7 This is the strongest dimension of the essay. The question asks "to what extent do you agree or disagree?" and the writer correctly adopts a partial agreement position, stated clearly in the introduction and reinforced in the conclusion. Both sides are addressed with relevant ideas: addiction and social isolation against stress relief, learning, and online connection. Ideas are reasonably extended with examples (teenagers neglecting homework, friends on phones in coffee shops, waiting for a bus). The main weakness is a slight imbalance: the "against" paragraph feels more vivid and convincing, while the "for" paragraph lists benefits rather loosely without deep development. The conclusion also slightly shifts in tone: moving from "not completely harmful" to "strongly believe it is very useful", which introduces minor inconsistency in the position (instead of "strongly believe it is very useful", say "believe it can be useful")
Coherence & Cohesion 6-7 The essay has a clear four-paragraph structure and logical overall progression. Cohesive devices are varied and generally appropriate: "Firstly," "Moreover," "In fact," "As a result," "Furthermore," "In addition," "In conclusion." However, some transitions are mechanical: "In addition" before the online friends point feels tacked on rather than logically connected to what came before. The paragraph on negative effects is internally well-sequenced (addiction → physical health → social isolation), but the positive paragraph reads more like a list than a cohesive argument. Reference and substitution are handled adequately with no major confusion.
Lexical resource 6 There is a reasonable range of vocabulary, with some good choices: "gradually isolated," "convenient way to reduce stress," "problem-solving," "real-life communication." However, the essay leans on general, safe vocabulary rather than pushing toward more precise or sophisticated expression. Phrases like "very popular," "very harmful," "very convenient," and "very useful" appear repeatedly. Overuse of "very" as an intensifier is a noticeable weakness at this level. Some more idiomatic or precise alternatives would elevate the score: for example, "sedentary lifestyle" instead of "harmful for their physical health," or "diminishes face-to-face interaction" instead of "do not want to communicate with others face to face." Spelling and word formation are clean throughout, which is a positive.
Grammatical range & accuracy 6-7 This is a solid performance grammatically. The essay uses a mix of simple and complex structures, and most are accurate. Good examples include relative clauses ("which is a modern way to connect with others"), conditional structures ("if people know how to limit their screen time"), and participial phrases ("watching a funny video or playing a short game"). The sentence "many teenagers spend all their free time looking at the screen instead of doing homework or playing outdoor sports which is very harmful for their physical health" has an ambiguous relative clause: "which" seems to refer to the entire preceding clause, making it slightly awkward. Overall, errors are minor and infrequent.
Overall estimated band6.5
Key areas to improve
  • Replace "very + adjective" combinations with stronger single-word alternatives (e.g., "extremely" → or better, a more precise adjective entirely). Develop the positive arguments more deeply — instead of listing three benefits briefly, pick two and explain each with a specific example or reasoning chain. Ensure the stated position in the introduction is consistent with the conclusion. Push vocabulary toward less common, more precise items to break into Band 7 on Lexical Resource.
