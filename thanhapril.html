Task

Scientists agree that many people eat too much junk food and it is damaging their health. Some people think that this problem can be solved by education, while others believe that this education will not work. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Essay

Some people believe that the problem when eating to much junk food can lead to their health condition, which can be solved by education, while others think this education will not work. I am partitial agree that education can help improve the awareness of people about food, but it is also neglected by who think health is not so important.

There are serveral reasons why people think education may affect positively in people decision, which is eating fast food can be harmful to their body. Firstly, the education is playing a vital role in building characteristic or good habit in children. When children go to school and living in different enviornment with friends and other people, they can easily be affected by other decisions like choosing toys, eating food,… Children may feel excited with fast food and street food after school to relief their stresses. That is why teacher should teach them about the food and the importance of eating healthy food. Secondly, there are a lot of community in both social media and reality encourgaging eating healthy food and avoiding junk food can brings significant result to people's health. In modern life, many people choose to buy food in convenience stores instead of eating at home due to the fast paced in big city. The class indoor or outdoor gradually become popular to bring people new insights into their daily lives. It can be another way to hold them back, help people stay calm and teach people take care of their helth more like eating healthy food. At the result, cooking classes are having more attendents recently not only learn how to make a new dishes but also is a way to take care of family and who they love around. Đoạn này đang cố gắng bao quát quá nhiều ý: giáo dục ở trường, ảnh hưởng từ bạn bè, mạng xã hội, lớp học nấu ăn và cả vấn đề căng thẳng - năm chủ đề khác nhau trong khi 1 đoạn văn chỉ nên xoay quanh 1 chủ đề chính. Nên chọn 1–2 ý mạnh nhất, trình bày rõ ràng, giải thích cụ thể và đưa ra ví dụ minh họa. Chất lượng triển khai quan trọng hơn số lượng ý tưởng.

However, the others think the education about food is meaningless in modern life. Many families decide to buy street food such as chicken fried or hambugers for their main meal instead of cooking at home due to many reasons. They are working far from home and the preparation for a meal at home may takes a lot of times, even if they know about the benefit of slow cooked foods. The education can be not work for people really do not care about their health condition. They enjoy eating junk food because it brings good taste, cheap price and feel more comfortable when eating at wherever they want. They can eat whatever they want at whenever in a day without wating for so long, which is a main reasons fast food restaurants open so many nowadays. People can know about the advantages of healthy food but they can not control what they want and what they learnt.

From my perspective, I would balance both views about the effection of education on how people change their health lifestyle. I would pay more attention about my health, and control what I am eating is good for my body, so I think the knowledge about food is so important to me. Meanwhiles, I also love the taste of fast food and the convenience its bring, so I sometimes eat them at the weekend or hang out with my friends. Ý kiến cá nhân trong phần kết luận mang tính cá nhân hóa quá mức và không hình thành được một lập luận mang tính khái quát (đề bài hỏi về mọi người chứ không hỏi về cá nhân bạn). Summary, I believe that the education about food can show deeper understand about healthy lifestyle and control the intake easier, but you do not need to follow it in a strict way, just be flexible on choosing your favorite food and your body health.

Task Response 5 The essay attempts to address both views and offers a personal opinion. Some relevant ideas are present on both sides — the role of school education, community influence, and convenience driving junk food choices are all pertinent points. However, the essay has significant task fulfilment issues. The personal opinion in the conclusion is highly personalised and informal ("I also love the taste of fast food... I sometimes eat them at the weekend"), which is inappropriate for an academic essay and fails to make a convincing general argument. The first body paragraph drifts considerably. It moves from school education to cooking classes to community groups without clear focus. Ideas are present but insufficiently developed and sometimes irrelevant, such as the discussion of cooking classes and stress relief. There is noticeable repetition across paragraphs. Read Nhat Huy's essay and see how he has developed two points for each side ("education is effective" and "education is ineffective").
Coherence & Cohesion 5 There is a recognisable four-paragraph structure and a general sense of progression. Cohesive devices are used — firstly, secondly, however, meanwhile — but with inconsistency and some misuse. "At the result" should be "As a result," and "Meanwhiles" is not a standard connector. The first body paragraph is particularly problematic in terms of internal organisation: it attempts to cover school education, peer influence, social media communities, and cooking classes all in one paragraph, with no clear topic sentence or logical thread connecting them. The second body paragraph is better focused. The conclusion abandons academic structure entirely and reads as a personal diary entry, which severely disrupts coherence at the essay level.
Lexical resource 5 There is some range of vocabulary and occasional attempts at less common items: vital role, insights, fast-paced, convenience stores, awareness. However, errors in word choice and word formation are frequent and noticeable. Key examples: "partitial agree" (partially agree), "serveral" (several), "enviornment" (environment), "encourgaging" (encouraging), "attendents" (attendees), "helth" (health), "hambugers" (hamburgers), "effection" (effectiveness), "wating" (waiting), "relief their stresses" (relieve their stress), "brings significant result" (yield significant results). These errors are consistent enough to cause some difficulty for the reader, though the general meaning remains mostly recoverable. Read Bình's essay, which scores 6.0 in Lexical Resources, to see how vocabulary can be used more effectively for this task.
Grammatical range & accuracy 5 There is some variety in sentence structures and the writer attempts complex constructions. Some of these work adequately, but many break down. Key problems: "eating to much junk food" (too much), "may takes a lot of times" (may take a lot of time), "the education can be not work" (education may not work), "a main reasons" (the main reason), "new dishes" (number inconsistency), "the fast paced in big city" (the fast pace of life in big cities). Subject-verb agreement errors are scattered throughout. The greatest accuracy is achieved in simple sentences; complex structures are frequently faulty. Punctuation is mostly adequate but the use of "…" is informal and inappropriate for academic writing. Read Bình's essay, which scores 6.5 in Grammatical range & accuracy, to see how grammar can be used more effectively and accurately for this task.

Key Priorities to Improve
  • Rewrite the conclusion academically. The conclusion must summarise your position on the general debate, not describe your personal eating habits. Replace "I also love the taste of fast food and sometimes eat it at the weekend" with a general statement about what society or individuals should do. Personal anecdotes are not appropriate in IELTS Task 2.
  • Focus each body paragraph on one clear idea. The first body paragraph tries to cover school education, peer influence, social media, cooking classes, and stress. This is five different topics. Choose the strongest one or two points, state them clearly, explain them, and give a specific example. Quality of development matters far more than quantity of ideas.
  • Target high-frequency spelling errors. Words like several, environment, health, hamburgers, encouraging appear misspelled and are common IELTS vocabulary. Systematic spelling practice on topic-specific word lists would directly raise the Lexical Resource score.
  • Eliminate informal features. Ellipsis ("…"), personal eating anecdotes, and conversational phrases like "just be flexible" and "who they love around" all lower the academic register. Aim for formal, impersonal language throughout.
