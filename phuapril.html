At the beginning, the video games could be played too much time by player which affect negatively to their health. All most the players are children or teenagers that are easy to immerse in the video games because it's was designed that attract young people to spend the time on sitting and focusing on the screen, which can lead to addict habits from playing video games. Furthermore, playing video games over the time can harm to personal reality relationships. If the individuals choose to stay alone and just play the video games, there is no time for them to get connection with each other from society that could be led to lack of social skills or soft skills.

However, the video games can bring some advantage benefits to individual if they play smartly. The main reason that video games is the entertainment, gaming may help people to release the stress after a long day in easy way. We only need simple devices at home to receive various entertainment without going out. Additionally, the video games can get connection with many players who might around the world, and help you to have more friends further from your place if you play online video game attach with the internet.

Inclusion, The video game can bring to us the both advantage or disadvantages and they base on our adjustment or purples to use it.

Task Response 5 The essay presents a partial agreement position and attempts to discuss both harmful and beneficial aspects of handheld gaming. However, "To what extent do you agree or disagree?" requires the writer to take a clear, defended stance — partially agreeing is acceptable, but the degree of agreement needs to be argued more precisely and consistently. Ideas are present but underdeveloped: the social skills point and stress relief point are relevant but neither is elaborated with sufficient depth or concrete support. The conclusion is extremely thin and fails to synthesise the argument meaningfully. Read Thảo's essay, which scores 6.0 in Task Response, to see how ideas can be better developed.
Coherence & Cohesion 4.5 There is a recognisable structure with an introduction, two body paragraphs, and a conclusion. However, internal paragraph logic is often unclear or jumpy. The opening of the second paragraph ("At the beginning") is an unusual and inappropriate discourse marker — this phrase is typically used for narratives, not academic arguments. Cohesive devices are limited and sometimes misused. Sentences within paragraphs are not always smoothly connected, and the reader must work to follow the intended logic. The conclusion is a single vague sentence with no real progression from the body.
Lexical resource 4 The vocabulary range is limited and frequently imprecise. There are multiple word choice errors that impede meaning: "popular reason to effect" (should be common cause of harm), "addict habits" (addictive habits), "over the time" (over time), "harm to personal reality relationships" (unclear — real-life relationships?), "advantage benefits" (redundant), "purples" (purposes), "Inclusion" (In conclusion). Spelling errors are noticeable: "Nowaday," "addict," "purples." The writer occasionally attempts less common items (immerse, adjustment) but these are inconsistently controlled. Repetition of video games throughout without variation further limits the score.
Grammatical range & accuracy 4 Simple sentences are attempted but frequently contain errors. Complex structures are tried but tend to break down: "are believed that is one of popular reason," "it's was designed that attract young people," "can harm to personal reality relationships," "could be led to lack of", "the video games can get connection with". Subject-verb agreement is inconsistent ("video games is"). There is frequent confusion between verb forms, articles, and prepositions throughout. Errors are numerous enough to sometimes obscure meaning, though the general intent of most sentences can usually be understood. Read Thảo's essay, which scores 6 in Grammatical range & accuracy, to see how grammar use can be developed for this topic.
Overall estimated band4.5
Key Issues to Address
  • Grammar fundamentals are still inconsistent. Subject-verb agreement, article use (a/an/the), and preposition choice (effect on / affect, harm to) break down frequently. These are the same patterns from your earlier essays, which means they need focused, deliberate practice rather than just more writing.
  • Avoid filler and vague phrases. Phrases like "At the beginning," "advantage benefits," "base on our adjustment or purples" add no meaning and signal to the examiner that vocabulary and structure are being stretched beyond control. It is better to write a simpler, correct sentence than a complex, broken one.
  • Develop each point more fully. Each body paragraph currently lists two points shallowly. It is more effective at this level to develop one point per paragraph deeply — state it, explain the mechanism, give a specific example, and link back to the question.
  • Write a proper conclusion. Your conclusion should restate your position and briefly summarise your main reasons in one or two complete, accurate sentences — not a single vague line.
