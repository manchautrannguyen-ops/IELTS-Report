Task

Scientists agree that many people eat too much junk food and it is damaging their health. Some people think that this problem can be solved by education, while others believe that this education will not work. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Essay

Many scientists have agreement that many individuals is addicted to fast food too much and that could be harmful for health. Many people believe that the reason can be solved by education. On the other hand, many individuals think that the education will not work. This passage will discuss both of views.

Firstly, the problem could not be solved by education. Many people have this opinion because they believe that the differences in how people thinking. In many countries, with the same similarity in education but some people get many diseases while others choose to not consuming too much fast food to protect their health. Also, another reason is many people choose cosuming too much like how they can reduce the stress. In many developed countries, with the good education standard, they still have a large number of people use junk food to reduce the stressful feeling. Triển khai ý trong đoạn này rất rối và khó hiểu. Giáo dục thất bại vì mỗi người suy nghĩ khác nhau là đúng, nhưng ví dụ "some people get many diseases while others choose not to consume too much fast food" chưa đủ thuyết phục để chứng minh giáo dục không hiệu quả. Cần tổ chức lại đoạn này xoay quanh một hoặc hai lý do hợp lý để giải thích vì sao giáo dục không thể thay đổi hành vi. Ví dụ: thói quen lâu năm rất khó thay đổi, hoặc lựa chọn thực phẩm phụ thuộc vào giá cả và thời gian hơn là kiến thức.

In contrast, many people believe that education could be a great solution for reducing junk food. Nowadays, many schools in the world are having health checking for student in many occasion in a year. Also, with checking health, schools can invite many doctor to teach about the unhealthy lifestyle of using too much fast food that can help students have knowledge to protect their health. For example, many countries in the world reduced obisety rate by teaching civilians soon in school. Also, with internet, governments can have many news or video to teach their civilians about the harmfulness of junk food with their health. With that, people can learn how to protect their health and have a good knowledge to deal with disease like obesity.

Overall, I think that the education can be a key to solve the junk food consuming problem. Many fast food like snack or burger can be tasty but they need to be consumed with a knowledge, because it can have harmfulness to people's health if it is consumed too much.

CriterionScoreComment
Task Response 4–5 Both views are present and a personal opinion is given in the conclusion, satisfying the basic structural requirements. However, the development of the "education cannot work" paragraph is muddled. The argument opens with the claim that education fails because people think differently, but then illustrates this with "some people get many diseases while others choose not to consume too much fast food", which does not logically demonstrate that education is ineffective. The stress-relief point is relevant but undeveloped. The "education can work" paragraph is more coherent, raising school health checks, doctor visits, and government campaigns as credible examples. The personal opinion is stated but not defended. The conclusion simply restates the topic rather than explaining why education is the key. Overall, the task is addressed but only incompletely, with uneven development between the two sides.
Coherence & Cohesion 5 There is a clear four-paragraph structure: introduction, two body paragraphs, and a conclusion, and the overall progression from one side to the other is broadly logical. Discourse markers are used throughout: "Firstly," "Also," "In contrast," "For example," "With that," and "Overall" all contribute to a sense of organisation. However, several issues limit the score. Within the first body paragraph, the logical connection between sentences is weak. The relationship between ideas has to be inferred rather than being made explicit. "In many countries, with the same similarity in education but some people get many diseases" lacks a clear connective link to the topic sentence. "This passage will discuss both of views" in the introduction is a formulaic filler that contributes nothing to cohesion. In the second body paragraph, "Also" is used twice in close succession, which creates a mechanical feel. Substituting one with "Furthermore" or "Moreover" would improve cohesive variety. "With that" as a connector is somewhat vague. "As a result" or "Consequently" would link ideas more precisely. Paragraphing is present and broadly purposeful, but the central argument within the first body paragraph is not always clear to the reader, which limits overall coherence. These features are consistent with Band 5.
Lexical resource 5 There is some attempt at varied vocabulary: "harmfulness," "unhealthy lifestyle," "obesity rate," "civilians," "stressful feeling", but the range is limited and several word choices are inaccurate or repetitive. "Many scientists have agreement" (should be "many scientists agree"), "addicted to fast food too much" (awkward: "consuming too much junk food"), "with the same similarity" (redundant: "similarity" already implies sameness), "not consuming / consuming" repeated many times, "obisety" (obesity — misspelled), "deal with disease like obesity" (should be "address health issues such as obesity"), "with checking health" (awkward: "alongside health check-ups"). The word "consuming" appears many times. This repetition is consistent with Band 5.
Grammatical range & accuracy 4–5 There is a mix of simple and complex sentences, and some structures are handled okay: "with the good education standard, they still have a large number of people" shows an attempt at complex construction, and "schools can invite many doctors to teach about the unhealthy lifestyle" is largely correct. However, errors are frequent enough to cause some difficulty. Key issues include: "many individuals is addicted" (subject-verb agreement: "are addicted"), "in how people thinking" (should be "in how people think"), plural nouns ("for student," "invite many doctor"), and "This passage will discuss both of views" (should be "both views"). Punctuation is generally adequate. The level of accuracy on complex structures is inconsistent, matching Band 5.
Overall estimated band4.5
Key areas to improve
  • Restructure the first body paragraph around one or two clear, logical reasons why education alone cannot change behaviour. For example, that deep-rooted habits are hard to break, or that food choices are driven by affordability and time rather than knowledge. Remove the self-contradictory sentence about people choosing not to eat junk food, as it undermines the argument.
  • Replace repeated "Also" with varied connectors such as "Furthermore" or "Moreover."
  • Fix the recurring subject-verb agreement pattern. Practise: "many people are," "education does not work," "it depends."
  • Spell-check "consuming," "obesity," and "occasions" before submitting.
