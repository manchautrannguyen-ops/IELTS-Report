Task

Scientists agree that many people eat too much junk food and it is damaging their health. Some people think that this problem can be solved by education, while others believe that this education will not work. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Essay

Researchers accept that some people eat too much fast food and it is harming their health. I believe that this problem can be solved by education but it won't work with the false education. Bài viết này đã hiểu sai đề khi biến hai quan điểm thành "giáo dục đúng" và "giáo dục sai". Trong khi đó, đề bài gốc chỉ yêu cầu xem xét liệu giáo dục có hiệu quả hay không, chứ không phải đánh giá chất lượng của nó.

On the one hand, true education can bring many effective solutions to solve the harmful food problem. Firstly, school should promote healthy food in students' portion. For example, food like vegetables and white meat should be increased while limiting junk food such as fried chicken and pizza. Therefore, students will learn how to live in healthy way. Secondly, family could teach their children about the negative impact of junk food on their health. Family can show the image of obesity, diabetes and health problems to enhance the children' awareness. From that, the children will be afraid and avoid junk food.

On the other hand, false education can bring many negative effects and can't solve the harmful food problem. Firstly, family don't control their children portion. As a result, they eat fast food freely since young age and develop this bad hobby. Secondly, the misunderstanding and beautiful image of fast food of advertisement can attract the teenager. They imagine the fast food isn't leave any bad impact on their health. This will make them to be addicted to fast food and leave many bad impact. Đoạn này nhằm phản bác "giáo dục sai" chứ không chứng minh được quan điểm rằng giáo dục không hiệu quả; hai luận điểm về việc nuôi dạy con không tốt và quảng cáo mới là vấn đề là một ý khác so với yêu cầu ban đầu của đề bài.

In conclusion, fast food is very harmful for people's health. Everyone should eat healthy food regularly and limit eating fastfood to control the addiction as a true education.

Task Response 5 The task asks the writer to discuss two specific views: that education can solve the junk food problem, and that it cannot, and then give a personal opinion. This essay reframes those two views as "true education" versus "false education," which is a significant misreading of the task. The original question presents education as either effective or ineffective; it does not ask about the quality of education itself. As a result, the second body paragraph arguing against "false education" does not actually defend the view that education cannot work. It argues instead that bad parenting and advertising are problems, which is a different point entirely. The position stated in the introduction is also somewhat confused: "it can be solved by education but it won't work with false education" conflates the two views rather than presenting them distinctly. Some relevant ideas appear (school meals, family guidance, advertising influence) but they are not consistently tied back to the actual debate in the task. Read Bình's essay, which scores 6.5 in Task Response, to see how to develop relevant ideas for this task.
Coherence & Cohesion 5 There is a recognisable structure with an introduction, two body paragraphs, and a conclusion, and basic sequencing devices are used ("Firstly," "Secondly," "As a result," "Therefore," "From that"). However, cohesion within paragraphs is inconsistent. "From that, the children will be afraid and avoid junk food" is an awkward linking phrase. "As a result" or "consequently" would be more natural. Paragraphing is present and broadly logical, though the central focus of each paragraph is not always sharp.
Lexical resource 5 There is some reasonable vocabulary, such as "obesity," "diabetes," "awareness," "addicted," "negative impact", but the range is limited and repetition is frequent. "Fast food," "junk food," and "harmful food" are used interchangeably without clear distinction, and "harmful food problem" is an awkward coinage. "Bad hobby" is an inaccurate word choice; "habit" is the correct term here. "students' portion" should be "students' meals" or "students' diet". "Beautiful image of fast food" is imprecise; something like "appealing portrayal" or "glamourised image" would be more accurate. "Leave many bad impact" (used twice) is both grammatically incorrect and repetitive. The conclusion introduces "fastfood" as one word, which is non-standard. Word choice errors are noticeable but meaning generally remains clear. Read Bình's essay, which scores 6.5 in Lexical Resources, to see how vocabulary can be used more effectively for this task.
Grammatical range & accuracy 4–5 Simple sentences make up the majority of the essay, and complex structures, when attempted, are often faulty. Key errors include: "live in healthy way" (missing article: "in a healthy way"), "family don't control" (subject-verb agreement: "families don't" or "the family doesn't"), "the misunderstanding and beautiful image of fast food of advertisement" (awkward and grammatically unclear), "make them to be addicted" (incorrect infinitive construction: "make them addicted"), "leave many bad impact" (should be "leave many bad impacts" or "have a negative impact"). Punctuation is generally adequate. The frequency of these errors, particularly agreement and article omissions, is characteristic of Band 4–5. Key areas to improve
  • Re-read the task question carefully: the two views are about whether education works or doesn't work, not about good versus bad education. Every body paragraph must engage with that specific debate.
  • Replace "bad hobby" with "bad habit". This is a fixed collocation.
  • Work on subject-verb agreement, especially with collective nouns like "family."
  • Avoid translating connectors directly. "From that" is not natural in English; use "consequently," "as a result," or "therefore" instead.
  • The most urgent fix for this writer is task understanding. The "true vs false education" reframing is the single biggest factor holding the score down. The ideas themselves are not bad, but they need to be redirected to answer the actual question.
