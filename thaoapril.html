Task

The availability of entertainment such as video games on handheld devices are harmful to individuals and to the society they live in. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Essay

Many people think that the availability of entertainment such as video games on handheld devices can harm not only to individuals but also to the society they live in. However, it can be quite useful for young people in some aspects if they know how to control it carefully.

First, this type of entertainment also helps not only students but also adults reduce the stress of studying and working. Because of its convenience, people can play games whenever they have free time anywhere such as students can play video games with their friends in the coffee shop or officers can also entertain during their breaktime. This time will distract them from stress and come back with a better condition. Many mobile video games can also help people develop their thinking and creativity. These games require players to understand the rules of the game clearly and then increase their logical thinking.

On the other hand, if people, especially young people, are too deep into video games on handheld devices, it can cause many significant consequences for them. When they spend too much time playing mobile games, they become more addicted to electronic devices. They want to spend most of their time playing games. As a result, their productivity will be decreased and it can cause many health issues such as eye strain,... Moreover, this availability of entertainment for these games can also affect society in bad aspects. Many children, without their parent's control, can access games with violence content. Because of being in the young age without knowledge, they become deeper into the character in the games and as a result, they behave with violent actions. Therefore, people have to control their mind themselves, not being addicted to video games and spend too much time on this type of entertainment. Along with that, parents and schools also have to warn children about the negative effects of video games on handheld devices by having some workshops or explanations for them. Đoạn này khá dài và chuyển qua lại giữa nhiều ý nhỏ mà thiếu sự liên kết, dẫn dắt rõ ràng. Các câu mang tính đề xuất/giải pháp ở cuối đoạn cũng chưa phù hợp vì phần giải pháp nên đặt ở kết luận hoặc một đoạn riêng, thay vì chen vào giữa phần lập luận.

Although video games on handheld devices may be harmful to individuals and to society if they are addicted to it, it can be useful for relaxing and logical thinking if people know how to control it carefully.

Hướng cải thiện
  • Học viên cần trả lời trực tiếp vào 'to what extent'. Dạng này yêu cầu nêu rõ mức độ đồng ý/không đồng ý, ví dụ như 'I largely disagree, as the benefits outweigh the harms when usage is moderated'. Quan điểm này nên xuất hiện ngay từ mở bài và được nhấn mạnh lại ở kết luận. Bài hiện tại vẫn né việc đưa ra lập trường.
  • Đoạn thân thứ hai cần được tổ chức chặt hơn. Hiện đang gom quá nhiều ý vào một đoạn (nghiện, sức khỏe, bạo lực, trách nhiệm phụ huynh, giải pháp). Nên tách ra thành các ý rõ ràng, hoặc chọn 1–2 ý mạnh nhất rồi phát triển kỹ. Một ý làm sâu sẽ tốt hơn nhiều ý nhưng nông.
  • Giảm lặp từ. Cụm 'video games on handheld devices' đang bị lặp lại nhiều lần, có thể thay bằng 'mobile gaming', 'handheld entertainment', 'these platforms', 'such applications' để đa dạng hơn.
  • Tránh các cách diễn đạt mang tính informal. Những kiểu như '...', 'along with that', hay 'too deep into' làm giảm độ học thuật của bài. Nên dùng từ nối và cách diễn đạt chính xác, trang trọng hơn.
CriterionScoreComment
Task Response 6 The essay presents a clear position (partial disagreement) and addresses both harmful and beneficial aspects. However, there is a significant task fulfilment issue: the question asks to what extent the writer agrees or disagrees, which requires a clearly defended personal stance. This essay leans heavily toward discussing both sides in a balanced way, making it read more like a "discuss both views" response than a direct opinion essay. The position — that games are fine if controlled — is present but not forcefully argued. Ideas are relevant and some are developed, but the benefits side (stress relief, logical thinking) receives noticeably less depth than the harms side. The use of "..." is inappropriate in formal academic writing.
Coherence & Cohesion 6 There is a clear overall structure and logical progression from introduction through two body paragraphs to a conclusion. Cohesive devices are used with reasonable effect: however, as a result, moreover, therefore, along with that. However, there are some mechanical or awkward transitions — "along with that" is informal and abrupt, and the second body paragraph becomes quite long and shifts between multiple sub-points (addiction, health, violence, parental control, solutions) without clear internal signposting. The recommendation sentences at the end of the second body paragraph feel misplaced — solutions belong in a conclusion or a dedicated paragraph, not mid-argument.
Lexical resource 6 The vocabulary range is generally adequate for the task. The writer uses some appropriate topic-specific terms: addicted, productivity, eye strain, violence content, logical thinking, electronic devices. There is some flexibility in expression, though the range is somewhat restricted and repetitive — video games on handheld devices appears in nearly identical form five or six times. Some word choices are imprecise or unnatural: "too deep into video games" (too absorbed in / overly immersed in), "harm not only to individuals" (be harmful not only to), "officers" (the intended meaning appears to be office workers), "games with violence content" (violent content), "distract them from stress", "affect society in bad aspects". Spelling is clean with no noticeable errors, which is a positive.
Grammatical range & accuracy 6 There is a mix of simple and complex structures, and complex sentences generally hold together adequately. Some variety is evident through relative clauses and conditional structures. However, errors occur with enough frequency to be noticed. Key issues: "harm not only to individuals" (grammatically broken — be harmful not only to), "such as students can play video games" (such as does not precede a clause), "their productivity will be decreased" (reduced is more natural; passive is clunky here), "without knowledge" (without sufficient understanding/awareness would be more precise), "spend too much time on this type of entertainment" repeats without grammatical variation. Overall, errors occur but rarely impede communication.
Overall estimated band6.0
What Would Push This to Band 7
  • Respond directly to "to what extent." This question type requires a clearly stated degree of agreement or disagreement — for example, "I largely disagree, as the benefits outweigh the harms when usage is moderated." State this in the introduction and reinforce it in the conclusion. The current essay avoids committing clearly enough.
  • Structure the second body paragraph more tightly. It currently covers addiction, health problems, violence, parental responsibility, and solutions all in one paragraph. Split these into focused points, or select the two strongest and develop them properly. One well-developed point is stronger than four shallow ones.
  • Reduce repetition of key phrases. Replace repeated instances of "video games on handheld devices" with mobile gaming, handheld entertainment, these platforms, such applications to demonstrate lexical range.
  • Avoid informal markers. "...", "along with that", and "too deep into" lower the register. Academic writing benefits from more precise, formal connectors and word choices.
